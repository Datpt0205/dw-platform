# Platform glossary

This is the platform context's glossary: the shared vocabulary of the platform ADRs (`docs/adr/0100`–`0127`) and the Studio planning specs and tickets under `.claude/plans/studio/`. Studio is platform code, not a separate bounded context (ADR-0100), so its terms belong here — in the platform `CONTEXT.md` that `CONTEXT-MAP.md` names, never a `GLOSSARY.md` (`docs/agents/domain.md`).

**UI text uses the Vietnamese term, never the English one.** Each entry gives the English name in bold (for code comments, docs and discussion), its `code name` in backticks (what code and the schema use), and the Vietnamese UI label in italics (what the product shows a user). A term marked _(internal)_ has no UI label because users never see it. _Owner_ names the spec under `.claude/plans/studio/` (or the platform) that owns the term; _Tránh_ lists the synonyms not to use.

## Platform terms (already in the repository)

- **Tenant** (`tenant_id`) · _Công ty_ — One customer of the deployment; the outer isolation boundary enforced by PostgreSQL RLS on `app.tenant_id`. _Owner_: platform. _Tránh_: organisation (in code), customer (in code), tổ chức (in UI).
- **Workspace** (`workspace_id, platform.workspaces`) · _workspace_ — A data-owning business unit of a tenant (company, division, department) that may nest in a tree (D1); records carry an owning workspace and roles reach across workspaces by depth, never by copying. _Owner_: org-and-principals. _Tránh_: department (as the term), business unit (in code), environment, Không gian làm việc, Phòng ban, Đơn vị (as the UI term).
- **Root workspace** (`parent_id IS NULL`) · _Workspace gốc_ — The top of a tenant's workspace tree, usually the company itself. _Owner_: org-and-principals. _Tránh_: main workspace, default workspace.
- **Workspace sub-tree** (`workspace_subtree`) · _Workspace và các workspace con_ — A workspace together with every workspace below it in the tree, at most six levels deep (recommended). _Owner_: org-and-principals. _Tránh_: branch, division tree, descendants (in UI).
- **Archived workspace** (`archived_at`) · _Workspace đã lưu trữ_ — A workspace closed to new records and assignments whose existing records keep their owning workspace. _Owner_: org-and-principals. _Tránh_: deleted workspace, disabled workspace.
- **Tenant-wide table** (`_TENANT_WIDE_ON_PURPOSE`, `owning_workspace_id`) · _Bảng toàn tenant_ — A tenant table with `tenant_id` and no `workspace_id`, because its rows belong to the whole tenant: such as a Studio definition, the tenant's members, roles and access versions, its configuration, limits and counters, its consent and suppression lists, a content-addressed manifest, or a child row whose parent carries the workspace. Its RLS narrows by tenant only; when one workspace owns a row, `owning_workspace_id` names it for authorization, never for RLS. `test_rls_coverage.py` lists every such table with its reason and fails on one it does not list (ADR 0100). _Owner_: platform. _Tránh_: global table, shared table, unscoped table.
- **Membership** (`platform.memberships`) · _Thành viên workspace_ — A user's presence in one workspace; role assignments anchor to it. _Owner_: tenant-rbac. _Tránh_: seat, enrolment.
- **Tenant member** (`tenant_members`) · _Thành viên công ty_ — A user who belongs to a tenant at all, independent of any workspace membership. _Owner_: org-and-principals. _Tránh_: employee, staff, user account (in UI).
- **Tenant admin** (`holder of platform.tenant.* permissions`) · _Quản trị viên công ty_ — A tenant member holding the permissions to administer the tenant's configuration. _Owner_: tenant-rbac. _Tránh_: super user, owner (unless tenant_owner).
- **Operator** (`platform operator, support staff`) · _Người vận hành_ — Staff of the platform provider; never reads a tenant's business data except through customer-granted support access (ADR 0024), and never holds `tenant_owner`, `studio.publish.approve` or `platform.roles.write` in a customer's tenant, which PostgreSQL refuses, so an operator is never one of a new tenant's two owners (see Tenant setup state, V4.38). Changed 2026-10-25 and flagged for `mattpocock-skills:domain-modeling`. _Owner_: telemetry-and-audit. _Tránh_: admin (alone), platform admin.
- **Role** (`tenant_role (kind='role'), platform.roles`) · _Vai trò_ — A named bundle of permissions, each with an access depth (Dataverse security role); never a position in a hierarchy. _Owner_: tenant-rbac. _Tránh_: profile, position, hierarchy node, group.
- **Permission** (`permission_key (legacy identifier: scope)`) · _Quyền_ — One key of the permission catalogue, such as `studio.agents.read`, or `obj.mkt_lead.read` among the keys the catalogue derives from a live object definition (ADR 0104); granted only through roles. A `studio.*` key has exactly three segments, `studio.<area>.<verb>`, never four: its area is a plural noun when what it names is countable (`agents`, `workflows`, `objects`, `versions`, `bindings`, `tools`, `packs`, `evals`), and singular only for a reason (`publish`, an action; `lifecycle`, a process; `datahub`, a product name; `code`, an uncountable noun; `mcp`, a protocol name). tenant-rbac owns this rule and its coverage test checks it; it covers permission keys only, not approval types or audit event names. _Owner_: tenant-rbac. _Tránh_: scope (in new docs and UI), privilege (in code), right.
- **Permission catalogue** (`platform.permissions`) · _Danh mục quyền_ — The single code- and migration-owned list of permission keys with their scope kind and risk tier; tenants never author keys. _Owner_: tenant-rbac. _Tránh_: scope list, permission registry.
- **Permission set** (`tenant_role (kind='permission_set')`) · _Bộ quyền bổ sung_ — A role that is assigned in addition to a person's main role to add a few permissions; same table and semantics as a role. _Owner_: tenant-rbac. _Tránh_: profile, add-on role, extra role.
- **Role template** (`platform.roles row`) · _Vai trò mẫu_ — A platform- or pack-shipped role a tenant copies into its own roles. _Owner_: tenant-rbac. _Tránh_: default role, built-in role.
- **System role** (`is_system`) · _Vai trò hệ thống_ — A tenant role the platform keeps (such as tenant owner) that admins may assign but not edit or delete. _Owner_: tenant-rbac. _Tránh_: built-in role, locked role.
- **Tenant owner** (`tenant_owner`) · _Chủ sở hữu công ty_ — The system role that cannot lose its last holder and that holds, computed at read time and all at tenant depth, every active permission marked `tenant_grantable` (`platform.admin` is not), every enforced object action and every `fld.*` key on every live field; putting a `fld.*` key into any other role is still a role change that adds access, so it takes two people (ADR 0104). A tenant is created with two of them (see Tenant setup state, V4.38). Changed 2026-10-25 and flagged for `mattpocock-skills:domain-modeling`. _Owner_: tenant-rbac. _Tránh_: owner (alone), super admin.
- **Access depth** (`depth: own, team, workspace, workspace_subtree, tenant`) · _Mức truy cập: Của tôi / Của nhóm tôi / Workspace của tôi / Workspace của tôi và cấp dưới / Toàn công ty_ — How far one granted privilege reaches over records: records I own, my teams own, my workspace owns, my workspace sub-tree owns, or the whole tenant. _Owner_: tenant-rbac. _Tránh_: scope, level, range, Phạm vi (as the term), Phòng ban (as a value).
- **Role assignment** (`role_assignment`) · _Gán vai trò_ — One person (or team) holding one role, anchored at a workspace, optionally until an expiry. _Owner_: tenant-rbac. _Tránh_: grant (alone), binding, membership role_keys.
- **Anchor workspace** (`anchor_workspace_id`) · _Workspace được gán vai trò_ — The workspace a role assignment's depth is measured from. _Owner_: tenant-rbac. _Tránh_: home workspace, base workspace.
- **Acting workspace** (`AccessContext.workspace_id`) · _Workspace đang làm việc_ — The workspace the current request works in; it selects defaults, not the reach of effective permissions. _Owner_: tenant-rbac. _Tránh_: current workspace (in code), active workspace.
- **Effective permissions** (`platform.effective_grants`) · _Quyền hiệu lực_ — The union over all of a person's role assignments in the tenant of permission and depth, each depth anchored at its assignment's workspace. _Owner_: tenant-rbac. _Tránh_: computed scopes, resolved roles.
- **AccessContext** (`AccessContext`) · _(internal — not shown in the UI)_ — The verified server-side context of one request: tenant, acting workspace, principal and effective permissions; the only source of `app.tenant_id`. _Owner_: tenant-rbac. _Tránh_: session, request user, auth context.
- **Access version** (`access_version`) · _(internal — not shown in the UI)_ — A counter bumped when anything that changes effective permissions changes; part of the cached AccessContext key. _Owner_: tenant-rbac. _Tránh_: permission epoch (for this one), cache version.
- **Privilege escalation** (`rbac_escalation`) · _Leo thang quyền_ — Granting or editing a role so that someone gains a permission or depth the actor does not hold; refused by the hold rule. _Owner_: tenant-rbac. _Tránh_: escalation (alone), elevation.
- **Bind permission** (`platform.roles.bind`) · _Quyền gán vai trò_ — The permission to assign or remove roles the actor does not hold, except a role with a critical permission or with a field grant on a Sensitive field. _Owner_: tenant-rbac. _Tránh_: delegate admin, grant right.
- **Critical permission** (`risk='critical'`) · _Quyền trọng yếu_ — A permission in the catalogue's `critical` risk tier, such as `platform.roles.write` and `platform.roles.bind`, that the bind permission never lets anyone assign without holding it and that no service principal holds; a role with a field grant on a Sensitive field counts as critical for that rule. Granting one is a role change, so like every role change it takes two people from W1 (D11). _Owner_: tenant-rbac. _Tránh_: sensitive permission, admin permission.
- **Separation of duties** (`sod_rule`) · _Tách nhiệm_ — A rule that no one person may hold both sides of a pair of duties, evaluated per person across the tenant. _Owner_: tenant-rbac. _Tránh_: four-eyes (alone), SoD conflict list.
- **Waiver** (`sod_waiver`) · _Miễn trừ_ — A recorded, second-person-confirmed exception to a waivable separation-of-duties rule. _Owner_: tenant-rbac. _Tránh_: exemption, override.
- **Platform admin grant** (`platform_admin`) · _Quyền vượt của nền tảng_ — A platform-written, time-bound break-glass grant, retired once customer-granted support access ships. _Owner_: tenant-rbac. _Tránh_: platform_admin role (as a tenant role), god mode.
- **Run** (`platform.worker_runs, run_id`) · _Lượt chạy_ — One execution of a worker, agent or workflow version, admitted once, checkpointed and auditable. _Owner_: workflow-engine. _Tránh_: execution, job, instance, session.
- **Worker** (`configs/workers/<name>.yaml`) · _worker_ — A platform-defined, versioned digital worker configuration shipped in `configs/workers`; tenant-built ones are agents. _Owner_: studio-skeleton. _Tránh_: bot, assistant, Nhân sự số (as the code term).
- **Tool spec** (`ToolSpec, configs/tools`) · _Đặc tả công cụ_ — The versioned contract the ToolExecutor runs: schemas, scopes, side-effect level, approval policy, timeout, idempotency. _Owner_: tools-and-skills. _Tránh_: tool definition (for this), function schema.
- **ToolExecutor** (`ToolExecutor`) · _(internal — not shown in the UI)_ — The one place a tool call is authorized, validated, executed, output-validated and audited. _Owner_: tools-and-skills. _Tránh_: tool runner, tool wrapper.
- **Toolset** (`dw.toolset/v1, configs/toolsets`) · _Bộ công cụ_ — A versioned selection of tools offered to an agent together. _Owner_: tools-and-skills. _Tránh_: toolkit, tool bundle, plugin.
- **Side-effect level** (`side_effect_level`) · _Mức tác động_ — The tool contract's classification of what a call changes outside the run; drives approval and run-mode interception. _Owner_: tools-and-skills. _Tránh_: effect class (that is a connector action's `effect_class`), risk level.
- **Approval** (`platform.approval_requests`) · _Yêu cầu phê duyệt_ — A durable request for a human decision that pauses a run or, with no run, announces `<approval_type>.decided`. _Owner_: workflow-engine. _Tránh_: confirmation, sign-off, review (alone).
- **Approver** (`decided_by`) · _Người duyệt_ — The person who decides an approval; never the person who requested it where a strict prefix applies. _Owner_: workflow-engine. _Tránh_: checker (outside tenant config), reviewer.
- **Autonomy level** (`autonomy_level (A0 to A4)`) · _Mức tự chủ_ — How much a worker or agent may do without asking; the tenant ceiling only lowers it, never raises it. _Owner_: agent-builder. _Tránh_: automation level, trust level.
- **Tenant overlay** (`dw_kernel.overlay.TenantOverlay`) · _(internal — not shown in the UI)_ — The resolver that returns a tenant's own version of a platform artifact if one exists, else the platform's; never another tenant's. A Studio artifact has no platform layer and never resolves through it, and inside a Studio run the run manifest answers for platform artifacts too. _Owner_: tenant-config. _Tránh_: override layer, customization layer.
- **Release manifest** (`release manifest`) · _(internal — not shown in the UI)_ — The platform checkout's pinned set of worker, graph, prompt, tool, policy, schema and dataset versions; reads the platform layer only and is every run's pin of that layer, beside which a Studio run also pins its run manifest. _Owner_: telemetry-and-audit. _Tránh_: run manifest, deployment manifest.
- **Evaluation dataset** (`evals/datasets`) · _Bộ dữ liệu kiểm định nền tảng_ — A platform-shipped, versioned eval dataset with graders keyed `<context>.<gate>`. _Owner_: agent-lifecycle. _Tránh_: test set, golden set (alone).
- **Audit log** (`platform.audit_events`) · _Nhật ký kiểm toán_ — The append-only, partitioned record of who did what, written in the action's transaction. _Owner_: telemetry-and-audit. _Tránh_: activity log, history (alone), event log.
- **Model profile** (`profile_id, configs model profiles`) · _Hồ sơ model_ — A versioned, platform- or tenant-provided choice of model route and parameters an agent selects from what the plan allows; it carries no price, which only the price book holds. _Owner_: model-connections. _Tránh_: model config, LLM setting.
- **ModelGateway** (`ModelGateway`) · _(internal — not shown in the UI)_ — The platform port every model call goes through; the only place model routing, route class and metering apply. _Owner_: model-connections. _Tránh_: gateway (alone), LLM client.
- **Idempotency key** (`Idempotency-Key`) · _Khóa chống lặp_ — A caller-supplied key that makes a retried write happen once. An effect outside the platform is applied once by its provider only where its Action declares a key of its own or a lookup (see Delivery guarantee, V4.30); otherwise its delivery guarantee is unkeyed. Changed 2026-10-25 and flagged for `mattpocock-skills:domain-modeling`. _Owner_: app-standards. _Tránh_: request id, dedupe key (for this).
- **Composition root** (`bootstrap/wiring.py`) · _(internal — not shown in the UI)_ — The only place a concrete adapter is constructed and injected. _Owner_: studio-skeleton. _Tránh_: service locator, container (alone).
- **Seam** (`marked seam`) · _(internal — not shown in the UI)_ — A marked place in the composition roots where a capability or context plugs in. _Owner_: studio-skeleton. _Tránh_: hook, extension point.

## Studio core

- **Studio** (`studio, dw_studio`) · _Studio_ — The tenant-configurable no-code capability of the platform for building objects, workflows, agents and tools; a platform capability, not a bounded context. _Owner_: studio-skeleton. _Tránh_: builder, low-code app, Agent Studio (competitor name).
- **Studio capability** (`dw_studio.<capability>`) · _(internal — not shown in the UI)_ — One sub-package of the single `dw_studio` distribution, created together with its import contract (`core`, `records`, `workflows`, `agents`, `tools`, `lifecycle`, `testing`; W1 has only `core`). _Owner_: studio-skeleton. _Tránh_: capability (alone), module, feature.
- **Domain pack** (`configs/packs/<key>@<semver>`) · _Gói nghiệp vụ_ — A platform package for one business domain (Marketing first) whose components install into a tenant as forks with provenance. _Owner_: domain-packs. _Tránh_: template pack, app, marketplace item, solution.
- **Package** (`dw.package/v1`) · _Gói cài đặt_ — A content-addressed set of artifact documents, binding declarations, migrations and eval evidence; never records, secrets or history. _Owner_: domain-packs. _Tránh_: solution, bundle, export, Gói (alone).
- **Fork with provenance** (`origin`) · _Bản sao từ gói_ — A tenant artifact created from a pack component, or one the pilot seeded before the pack existed (such as `mkt_lead`) that installing the pack adopts, recording pack key, pack version, component key and base content hash; where an adopted artifact differs from the pack's version, the difference arrives as a pack update proposal into its draft. _Owner_: domain-packs. _Tránh_: clone, copy, instance.
- **Pack update proposal** (`pack_update_proposal`) · _Đề xuất cập nhật gói_ — A per-component three-way comparison whose application writes a draft, never a live version. _Owner_: domain-packs. _Tránh_: upgrade, auto-update, sync.
- **Reserved prefix** (`reserved_prefix (e.g. mkt_)`) · _Tiền tố dành riêng_ — An api-name prefix owned by a pack that tenant-created artifacts may not use. _Owner_: domain-packs. _Tránh_: namespace.
- **Builder** (`holder of an area's author key`) · _Người dựng_ — A business admin who builds objects, workflows and agents with no code (D2), through each area's author key: `studio.objects.manage`, `studio.workflows.edit`, `studio.agents.author`. _Owner_: studio-skeleton. _Tránh_: developer, maker (in UI), creator.
- **Author** (`contributor_ids plus submitter`) · _Tác giả_ — Everyone who edited or submitted a candidate version; no author may approve its publish. _Owner_: agent-lifecycle. _Tránh_: owner (for this), creator.
- **Studio artifact** (`studio.artifacts`) · _Thành phần_ — A tenant-built definition of one kind (object, workflow, agent, tool, skill, toolset, Data Hub endpoint, binding set) with an immutable key, an `api_name` and an owning workspace. _Owner_: tenant-config. _Tránh_: asset, resource, component (in code), template, artifact (alone, which means a platform artifact).
- **Artifact kind** (`kind, ArtifactKindRegistry`) · _Loại thành phần_ — A registered kind with document schema `dw.<kind>/v1`, validator, dependency extractor and publish hook, each supplied by the kind's owner; agent-lifecycle calls the hook inside its publish transaction (ADR 0109). _Owner_: tenant-config. _Tránh_: type (alone).
- **API name** (`api_name`) · _Tên API_ — The readable identifier of an artifact or field, unique per tenant and kind, immutable after the first publish. _Owner_: tenant-config. _Tránh_: key, slug, id (alone).
- **Version stage** (`stage: draft, evaluating, awaiting_approval, live, retired`) · _Giai đoạn_ — The one lifecycle state of an artifact version, guarded by `guard_version_stage()`; only one version per artifact is `live`. _Owner_: agent-lifecycle. _Tránh_: state, status (for this), active flag, Chặng (that is a record's Stage).
- **Draft** (`stage='draft'`) · _Nháp_ — The mutable version of an artifact, saved with optimistic concurrency; never run by a trigger or a `live` run. An artifact has at most one unpublished version, its draft or its candidate, beside at most one live version, so creating a draft while a candidate is pending is refused with 409 `conflict` and `details.reason_code` `candidate_pending`, and editing that candidate means withdrawing it to draft (ADR 0120). A `simulate` run, and a run of no other mode, may run it: `RunRequestService`, the one admission path, runs the checks that submit runs for the draft's kind over the draft's content itself, whichever route asked, and refuses the request on an error, which the test-run door answers with 422 and its `wf.*` codes (ADR 0109; ADR 0112 for a workflow, ADR 0117 for an agent); the run manifest pins a provisional hash of the draft's content at admission, computed as submit computes a version's hash, and records stage `draft`, so a resume after the draft has changed is refused with `incompatible_resume` (ADR 0109); and the run reads under a provisional boundary derived from the draft (see Boundary). _Owner_: tenant-config. _Tránh_: working copy, unpublished version (that is a draft or a candidate).
- **Candidate version** (`stage in (evaluating, awaiting_approval)`) · _Bản ứng viên_ — An immutable, numbered, hashed snapshot submitted from the draft and on its way through the quality gate and approval; while it is pending its artifact has no draft (see Draft). _Owner_: agent-lifecycle. _Tránh_: release, commit, snapshot.
- **Live version** (`stage='live'`) · _Đang dùng_ — The one version triggers and new runs of an artifact use, except that a `simulate` run (a gate run included) or, from W2, a `test` run may name another version, and only a `simulate` run may name the draft (see Draft). Only agent-lifecycle's `LifecycleService` makes a version live (see Publish). _Owner_: agent-lifecycle. _Tránh_: active version, current version, default version, Đang chạy (reserved for run status).
- **Retired version** (`stage='retired'`) · _Đã gỡ_ — A version no new run may use, kept while retained runs reference it. _Owner_: agent-lifecycle. _Tránh_: deleted, archived, deprecated.
- **Submit** (`submit`) · _Gửi kiểm định_ — Freezing the draft into a numbered candidate version and starting what its kind needs before publish: for an agent or workflow the capability analysis and the quality gate, for a kind without a gate the approval, and for an object definition version that only tightens, publish at once. _Owner_: agent-lifecycle. _Tránh_: publish (for this), gửi phát hành, save version.
- **Publish** (`publish`) · _Phát hành_ — Making a candidate version live after a second person approved it and, for an agent or workflow, its quality gate passed; agent-lifecycle's `LifecycleService` is the only writer of `live`, with `guard_version_stage()` as its backstop, and its publish transaction calls the kind's publish hook (ADR 0109). One branch skips the second person: an object definition version whose kind's comparator reports that every change against the live version only tightens (a field closed, default access narrowed, a permission removed) goes from draft to live at submit, by its submitter, with an audit event. The branch is the object kind's alone: a first version, any version that loosens anything and every version of another kind (a tool and a Data Hub endpoint included) take the approval (ADR 0120). _Owner_: agent-lifecycle. _Tránh_: activate, deploy, release, go live.
- **Withdraw** (`withdraw`) · _Rút lại_ — Returning a candidate version to the author before a decision. _Owner_: agent-lifecycle. _Tránh_: cancel, revert.
- **Canonical form** (`dw_kernel.canonical`) · _Dạng chuẩn_ — RFC 8785 JCS bytes of an NFC, float-free document; the only input to Studio hashes. _Owner_: tenant-config. _Tránh_: normalized JSON, sorted JSON.
- **Content hash** (`content_hash`) · _Mã băm nội dung_ — SHA-256 of the canonical bytes of one version's document. _Owner_: tenant-config. _Tránh_: checksum, fingerprint.
- **Definition hash** (`definition_hash`) · _Mã băm định nghĩa_ — SHA-256 of a version's content hash plus its pinned closure, fixed at submit; drift is keyed by it, evaluation evidence by the effective hash. _Owner_: tenant-config. _Tránh_: version hash, MCP definition hash.
- **Effective hash** (`effective_hash`) · _Mã băm hiệu lực_ — The definition hash plus the tenant-layer resolution of the platform artifacts it pins; evaluation evidence is keyed by it, so a tenant's own version of a pinned platform artifact makes earlier evidence void (ADR 0109). _Owner_: tenant-config. _Tránh_: runtime hash.
- **Dependency index** (`studio.dependencies`) · _Nơi đang dùng_ — Rows written at submit naming everything a version pins, binds, reads, writes or invokes; the one source for where-used, delete protection and drift. _Owner_: tenant-config. _Tránh_: reference table, usage table.
- **Impact analysis** (`impact analysis`) · _Phân tích ảnh hưởng_ — What breaks if a thing changes, read from the dependency index and open run manifests. _Owner_: tenant-config. _Tránh_: dependency check.
- **Drift** (`drift finding (incl. mcp_definition_changed)`) · _Lệch cấu hình_ — A live or approved thing whose pinned dependency, platform base, binding or external tool definition moved after it was approved. An MCP tool's drift is the finding `mcp_definition_changed` (ADR 0120); `mcp_tool_drifted` is not a finding but the code a call to that tool is refused with (ADR 0116). _Owner_: agent-lifecycle. _Tránh_: out of sync, stale config, definition drift (as a separate term), `mcp_tool_drifted` (for the finding).
- **Run manifest** (`run_manifest_hash, platform.run_manifests`) · _Bản kê lượt chạy_ — The content-addressed record of everything one Studio run resolved at admission: its root's definition hash (a draft's provisional hash for a `simulate` run of the draft, see Draft), the closure with its platform-layer components, the tenant config versions and binding entries it resolved (a connection by its id, the binding set by its version, a variable by the hash of its value), object schema versions and the engine with its `durability="sync"` (V4.34), never a secret and never a secret version: a call uses its connection's latest unrevoked secret version and records the one it used (V4.35; see Secret). The run resolves every artifact only from it, on resume too, and nothing falls back at run time; it is the run's second pin, beside the release manifest (ADR 0109). Changed 2026-10-25 and flagged for `mattpocock-skills:domain-modeling`. _Owner_: tenant-config. _Tránh_: release manifest, run snapshot.
- **Run mode** (`run_mode: live, test, simulate`) · _Chế độ chạy: Thật / Chạy thử / Mô phỏng_ — A server-set property of a run, fixed at admission and only safer in child runs, deciding which effects run, are intercepted or refused. A run in `simulate` is a _chạy mô phỏng_ in UI text, never _chạy thử_, which names mode `test` alone, and it is the only run that may run a draft (see Draft); a `trigger_kind` (such as `test`, which every run of the test-run door carries) says which door started a run, never its mode. _Owner_: tenant-config. _Tránh_: environment, dry run, sandbox mode, deployment profile, chạy thử (for a `simulate` run).
- **Test-run door** (`POST .../versions/{version_id}/test-runs`, `trigger_kind='test'`) · _Cửa chạy thử_ — The route a builder holding `studio.workflows.test` uses to ask for a run of one workflow version, a draft included in mode `simulate` alone (see Draft); it requests the run through `RunRequestService`, and every run it starts carries `trigger_kind` `test`. In W1 it takes mode `simulate` only and answers `test` with 422 until W2 opens the test recipient allowlist; it never takes `live` (422, ADR 0111). It names a door, not a mode: a run through it is a _chạy mô phỏng_ or, from W2, a _chạy thử_ by its run mode. Added 2026-10-24 and flagged for `mattpocock-skills:domain-modeling`. _Owner_: workflow-triggers. _Tránh_: test endpoint, sandbox run, chạy thử (for the door, or for a `simulate` run through it).
- **Intercepted call** (`tool_executions.status='simulated'`) · _Lệnh gọi giả lập_ — A tool call the executor did not perform because of the run mode; it returns a pinned mock or a schema placeholder. _Owner_: tenant-config. _Tránh_: mocked, skipped, fake call.
- **Mock output** (`mock`) · _Kết quả giả lập_ — A node's or tool's pinned sample output that an intercepted call returns. From W1 a scenario case of a workflow's quality gate pins one per step, the gate lane passes it into its trial, and in that `simulate` run the node wrapper applies it in place of the node type's own simulated answer once it validates against the step's output schema (V4.37, H-18), and refuses one for a `human.approval` step, whose simulated decision stays its only input (H-26); the builder's test-run door takes pinned outputs from W2. Without one, an intercepted call returns a placeholder generated from the output schema. Changed 2026-10-25 and flagged for `mattpocock-skills:domain-modeling`. _Owner_: workflow-nodes (the wrapper that applies it: workflow-engine). _Tránh_: stub, fixture (in UI).
- **Test recipient allowlist** (`studio.test_recipients`) · _Danh sách người nhận thử_ — Tenant-managed, expiring, normalised addresses that `test` runs may message for real. _Owner_: tenant-config. _Tránh_: whitelist, sandbox recipients.
- **Tenant config version** (`platform.config_versions`) · _Phiên bản cấu hình riêng_ — A tenant's own immutable version of a platform artifact, policy or binding set, in force only after a second person approves it; replaces `platform.policy_overrides`, which is removed at W2 once this table takes kind `policy`. _Owner_: tenant-config. _Tránh_: override (in UI), customization, patch.
- **Platform default** (`tenant_id=None layer`) · _Mặc định nền tảng_ — What ships in `configs/` and applies to a tenant without its own version. _Owner_: tenant-config. _Tránh_: system value, factory setting.
- **Base version** (`base_version`) · _Phiên bản gốc_ — The platform version a tenant config version was made from; a newer platform version is drift. _Owner_: tenant-config. _Tránh_: parent version.
- **Maker and checker** (`proposed_by, decided_by`) · _Người đề xuất, Người duyệt_ — The two different people D11 asks of a tenant change: every role change or assignment that adds access (one made with an invitation included), opening a basic personal-data field, widening an object's default access, a publish (an object definition's included, unless the version only tightens), a revive, lifting a tenant freeze, re-enabling a connection, adding an allowed host, an MCP tool approval, loosening the residency route and any other configuration change that loosens access. Tightening (narrowing access, closing a field, narrowing default access, soft-deleting a role, lowering a limit, tightening the residency route, a kill) takes one person and an audit event: close with one, open with two. Raising a limit and loosening the residency route have no two-person path at W1, so they stay locked there and the pilot's ceilings are set right when seeded. A role change reaches its checker through tenant-rbac's own proposal, applied in the checker's transaction (ADR 0104), never through a tenant config version. _Owner_: tenant-config. _Tránh_: four eyes, owner and approver.
- **Binding** (`bindings[] (kind connection, variable, principal, schedule)`) · _Điểm gắn_ — A named need a version declares whose value is tenant configuration, not content; values are approved separately and pinned per run. _Owner_: tenant-config. _Tránh_: connection reference, alias, env var, parameter, Liên kết.
- **Binding set** (`kind='binding_set'`) · _Bộ giá trị gắn_ — The approved values of one artifact's bindings in one tenant. _Owner_: tenant-config. _Tránh_: config file, settings.
- **Sandbox tenant** (`tenants.kind='sandbox' (v2)`) · _Công ty thử nghiệm_ — A separate tenant that only an operator creates and links to a production tenant, holding only synthetic data under its own limits and never running `live`. _Owner_: domain-packs. _Tránh_: sandbox (alone), staging, test workspace.
- **Promotion** (`studio.deployments (v2)`) · _Đưa lên_ — Importing a package from a sandbox tenant into the production tenant with re-gating and approval. _Owner_: domain-packs. _Tránh_: release, push, publish (for this).

## Identity and principals

- **Principal** (`principal_kind: user, service_principal, system_lane, support_staff`) · _(internal — not shown in the UI)_ — Whoever acts in a run, an audit event or a usage event: a user, a service principal, a platform lane (ADR 0011) or support staff (ADR 0024). An agent or a workflow is a boundary, never a principal, and a team owns records (`owner_kind`) and receives shares but never acts (ADR 0103). _Owner_: org-and-principals. _Tránh_: actor (for ownership), subject, team (as a principal kind).
- **Team** (`teams`) · _Nhóm_ — A set of tenant members in exactly one workspace that can own records and hold roles. _Owner_: org-and-principals. _Tránh_: group, squad, Đội.
- **Line manager** (`manager_user_id`) · _Quản lý trực tiếp_ — The person a member reports to; feeds hierarchy access and task escalation. The edit field keeps the existing label "Báo cáo cho". _Owner_: org-and-principals. _Tránh_: supervisor, boss, parent user.
- **Member attribute** (`member_attributes`) · _Thuộc tính nhân sự_ — An HR attribute of a tenant member (job code, branch, office) used by audiences. _Owner_: org-and-principals. _Tránh_: profile field, claim.
- **Audience** (`dw.audience/v1`) · _Người được dùng_ — The typed condition list deciding which members may use an agent or start a workflow. _Owner_: org-and-principals. _Tránh_: target group, Đối tượng sử dụng (clashes with Object).
- **Service principal** (`users.kind='service'`) · _Tài khoản dịch vụ_ — A non-human user row holding its own roles, used by unattended runs and API keys. _Owner_: org-and-principals. _Tránh_: bot user, system user, technical account.
- **Run-as principal** (`run_as (binding kind principal)`) · _Chạy dưới danh nghĩa_ — The principal whose permissions an unattended run uses; a service principal approved through a binding, never the publisher. _Owner_: org-and-principals. _Tránh_: run identity, impersonated user, Chạy với quyền của.
- **Accountable owner** (`owner_user_id (on an artifact)`) · _Người chịu trách nhiệm_ — The person answerable for a live agent or workflow; when they leave, publishing is blocked until reassigned. _Owner_: org-and-principals. _Tránh_: owner (alone), record owner.
- **API key** (`dwk_...`) · _Khóa API_ — A tenant-issued credential bound to a service principal. _Owner_: org-and-principals. _Tránh_: token (alone), secret key.
- **Login policy** (`sso_required`) · _Bắt buộc đăng nhập SSO_ — A tenant setting forcing members to sign in through the tenant's identity provider. _Owner_: org-and-principals. _Tránh_: SSO mode.
- **Invitation claim** (`invitation claim link`) · _Mã nhận lời mời_ — A one-time link that binds an invited person's identity to a tenant member. _Owner_: org-and-principals. _Tránh_: invite token, magic link.
- **Tenant setup state** (`platform.tenants.status='setup'`) · _Đang thiết lập_ — The status a new tenant is created in, beside `active`, `locked`, `offboarding` and `offboarded`: an operator's creation names two different owners, of whom the second may be a partner of the customer, invited as a member like any other, but never an operator (see Operator), and the tenant becomes `active` in the same transaction that activates the second owner (V4.38). Each time the active holders of `studio.publish.approve` or `platform.roles.write` drop below two, an alert goes to the tenant-depth holders of `platform.members.write`, and the operators' screen lists the tenants in `setup` and those below two (H-15). While a tenant is `setup` it behaves as `locked`, except the two owners' first sign-in and acceptance of the owner invitation and the operators' routes, and it leaves `setup` only for `active` or, through the operators' offboarding flow, `offboarding` (Đạt, Q-h1 and Q-h2, 2026-10-26). Added 2026-10-25 and flagged for `mattpocock-skills:domain-modeling`. _Owner_: tenant-rbac (activating a member: org-and-principals). _Tránh_: pending tenant, draft tenant, onboarding (alone), Đang chờ (that is a connection status).

## Record and field access

- **Record owner** (`owner_id, owner_kind (user or team)`) · _Người phụ trách_ — The user or team that owns a record; depth `own` and `team` are measured from it. _Owner_: record-access. _Tránh_: owner (alone), Người sở hữu, assignee.
- **Owning workspace** (`records.workspace_id`) · _Workspace sở hữu_ — The workspace a record belongs to; depth `workspace` and `workspace_subtree` are measured from it. _Owner_: record-access. _Tránh_: home workspace, Đơn vị sở hữu.
- **Default access** (`default_access: private, public_read, public_read_write`) · _Mặc định truy cập: Riêng tư / Mọi người được xem / Mọi người được xem và sửa_ — The per-object organisation-wide default deciding who beyond owner, depth and sharing reaches a record. _Owner_: record-access. _Tránh_: org-wide default (in UI), visibility.
- **Hierarchy access** (`grant_hierarchy`) · _Quản lý thấy bản ghi của cấp dưới_ — The per-object switch letting a line manager reach subordinates' records. _Owner_: record-access. _Tránh_: manager visibility, role hierarchy.
- **Share** (`studio.record_shares`) · _Chia sẻ_ — A manual, expiring grant of read or edit on one record to a user, team or workspace. _Owner_: record-access. _Tránh_: permission (for this), access grant.
- **Sharing rule** (`studio.sharing_rules`) · _Quy tắc chia sẻ_ — A versioned criteria-based rule granting read or edit on matching records to a principal set. _Owner_: record-access. _Tránh_: automatic share, access rule.
- **Record access** (`record_access_<table>_<cmd> policies`) · _Quyền trên bản ghi_ — The RLS policies combining owner, depth, hierarchy, sharing and default access for each command. _Owner_: record-access. _Tránh_: row security (alone), ACL.
- **Principal scope** (`studio.principal_scopes`) · _(internal — not shown in the UI)_ — The in-database closure of grant keys a principal reaches, rebuilt on an access epoch bump. _Owner_: record-access. _Tránh_: ACL cache, reach set.
- **Access epoch** (`studio.access_epochs`) · _(internal — not shown in the UI)_ — A per-tenant counter bumped when shares, rules, the tree or managers change, invalidating principal scopes. _Owner_: record-access. _Tránh_: access version (that is RBAC).
- **Actor kind** (`app.actor_kind`) · _Thực hiện bởi: Người dùng / Agent / Quy trình_ — The kind of actor a transaction runs for; selects the boundary applied over the principal's reach. _Owner_: record-access. _Tránh_: caller type.
- **Boundary** (`studio.actor_boundaries`) · _Giới hạn dữ liệu_ — The ceiling an agent or workflow version sets on what its run may reach, intersected with the reach of the principal the run acts as: its caller, or an unattended run's run-as principal (ADR 0103). A published version's boundary is fixed at publish. A `simulate` run whose root is not live, a draft (a dry evaluation's trial included) or a sealed candidate (a gate run's trial included), reads under a provisional boundary that `RunRequestService` derives from the root's closure by the same derivation the publish hook runs, intersected with the tester's access (ADR 0111) or, for a gate run's trial, with the access of the principal the gate lane runs as, recorded under the root's version id and pinned hash for that run alone and deleted when it ends; no `live` run ever uses a provisional boundary, which the database checks. The candidate arm is a provisional call of 2026-10-24 (G-1), since a candidate has no boundary before publish and a gate trial would otherwise read nothing; if it is wrong, a version's boundary is written when it is sealed instead. _Owner_: record-access. _Tránh_: sandbox, data scope.
- **Assign** (`studio.assign_record`, `POST .../records/{record_id}/assign`) · _Giao cho_ — Changing a record's owner, optionally moving its owning workspace, with audit; a sub-resource route (ADR 0108), never a `:assign` custom method. _Owner_: record-access. _Tránh_: transfer, reassign (in UI).
- **Explain access** (`explain endpoint`) · _Ai thấy bản ghi này_ — The view listing which arms (owner, depth, hierarchy, share, rule, default) give whom access to a record. _Owner_: record-access. _Tránh_: access report.
- **Field access mode** (`access_mode: open, secured`) · _Bảo mật trường: Mở / Bảo vệ_ — Whether a field is readable by everyone who reaches the record (open) or only by roles granted it (secured). _Owner_: field-security. _Tránh_: field visibility, hidden flag.
- **Field grant** (`studio.field_permissions.access: read, edit`) · _Xem / Xem và sửa_ — A role's `fld.<object>.<field>.read` or `.edit` permission on one secured field, applied to agents and search results too. _Owner_: tenant-rbac (the table and the role routes that grant it); field-security owns the one function that decides from it what a caller reads or edits. _Tránh_: column permission.
- **Hidden value** (`hidden_fields`) · _Đã ẩn_ — A field whose value the caller may not read: its name stays public and `hidden_fields` lists it on every read whether or not the value is empty, while the value never reaches the caller and cannot be filtered, sorted, counted or written (403 `field_not_editable`); screens show the marker with a lock, never a blank. _Owner_: field-security. _Tránh_: masked (for this), null.
- **Personal-data category** (`pd_category: none, basic, sensitive`) · _Loại dữ liệu cá nhân: Không / Cơ bản / Nhạy cảm_ — A field property under PDPL that drives field security, masking, routing, retention and data-subject requests. _Owner_: compliance-pdpl (the vocabulary, `dw_kernel.personal_data`, ADR 0106). _Tránh_: PII flag, classification (alone).
- **Subject identifier** (`subject_identifier`) · _Trường định danh chủ thể_ — A field that identifies the data subject a record is about, used to find records for a data-subject request. _Owner_: compliance-pdpl. _Tránh_: primary contact field.

- **Object** (`object_def`) · _Đối tượng_ — A tenant-defined record type (such as Lead) with fields, tenant-wide definition and per-object default access. _Owner_: custom-objects. _Tránh_: entity, table, custom object (in UI), DataTable.
- **Field** (`field_def`) · _Trường_ — One typed attribute of an object. _Owner_: custom-objects. _Tránh_: column, property, attribute.
- **Field key** (`field_defs.id`) · _Mã trường_ — The immutable internal identifier of a field; the API name is its readable twin. _Owner_: custom-objects. _Tránh_: field id (in UI).
- **Record** (`record, studio.records`) · _Bản ghi_ — One instance of an object, with owner, owning workspace and version. _Owner_: custom-objects. _Tránh_: row, item, entry.
- **Record name** (`name`) · _Tên bản ghi_ — The display name of a record used in lookups and search. _Owner_: custom-objects. _Tránh_: title, label.

- **Indexed field** (`slot-backed field`) · _Trường lọc được_ — A field stored in an indexed slot so it can be filtered and sorted; limited per object by plan. _Owner_: custom-objects. _Tránh_: searchable field, key field.
- **Slot** (`t07, i03`) · _(internal — not shown in the UI)_ — A typed physical column of `studio.records` that carries one indexed field. _Owner_: custom-objects. _Tránh_: column (in docs about fields).
- **Picklist** (`picklist, option key`) · _Danh sách chọn_ — A field whose value is one of a fixed list of options keyed by stable option keys. _Owner_: custom-objects. _Tránh_: enum, dropdown.
- **Lookup** (`lookup`) · _Trường tham chiếu_ — A field referencing a record of another object. _Owner_: custom-objects. _Tránh_: foreign key (in UI), reference (alone).

- **External ID** (`external_id`) · _Mã ngoài_ — A unique field holding an identifier from another system, an upsert key from W2. _Owner_: custom-objects. _Tránh_: source id, legacy id.
- **Upsert** (`upsert`) · _Tạo hoặc cập nhật_ — Creating a record or updating the one matched by a unique field: at W1 a unique field such as a normalised phone number or email, from W2 an external ID too. _Owner_: custom-objects. _Tránh_: merge, sync.
- **Validation rule** (`validation_rule (CEL)`) · _Điều kiện để lưu được_ — A CEL condition that must hold for a record to be saved (true means valid). _Owner_: custom-objects. _Tránh_: error condition, check (alone), Quy tắc kiểm tra.

## Workflows

- **Workflow** (`dw.workflow/v1, kind='workflow'`) · _Quy trình_ — A tenant-built, versioned document of `triggers[]` and a tree of steps (`steps[]`), each step holding one lane per port its type declares, compiled per version to a LangGraph StateGraph and run by the runner; the tree is the whole topology, so the document stores no edges and no canvas positions (V4.24, ADR 0112). _Owner_: workflow-engine. _Tránh_: flow, automation, scenario, pipeline, graph (for the document).
- **Stage** (`status field option`) · _Chặng_ — One option of the picklist an object uses as its status field. A long process is the object's lifecycle through its Stages: entering a Stage starts one or a few short workflows through a record event, so no run outlives its deadline, and a sub-workflow is only for reuse. The record alone holds its Stage; records list by Stage, and a Stage label covers contiguous steps of one sequence and only groups and collapses them on the canvas, with no run meaning (W2, ADR 0126). _Owner_: workflow-engine. _Tránh_: Giai đoạn (that is a version stage), `stage` as a code name (the version stage's column), phase, state.
- **Node** (`steps[]`) · _Bước_ — One step of a workflow's step tree (`{id, type, config, retry, timeout_s, on_error, description}`), of a node type, with at most one side effect through the ToolExecutor. Where a step sits in the tree is what connects it, and a step whose type declares ports holds one lane per port (see Port, Lane); the document field is `steps[]` from V4.24 (ADR 0112). _Owner_: workflow-nodes. _Tránh_: block, khối (as the UI term), action (for this), task.
- **Node type** (`type@major`) · _Loại bước_ — A catalogue entry defining a node's configuration schema, ports and behaviour. _Owner_: workflow-nodes. _Tránh_: node kind, operator.
- **Port** (`ports`) · _Nhánh ra_ — A named lane that a node type declares and a step of that type holds in the document (see Lane): each case of a `logic.switch` and its mandatory default _Khác_, the approval outcomes _Duyệt_ and _Từ chối_ (and _Hết hạn_ from W2), each lane of a parallel step, a loop step's body and its `max_reached` lane _Đã đủ N lần_, a map body (W2), and the error lane _Khi lỗi_ when `on_error` is `route`. A port holds steps, not an edge: its lane closes at the end of its step, where the step's own sequence continues (V4.24, ADR 0112). Changed 2026-10-24 and flagged for `mattpocock-skills:domain-modeling`. _Owner_: workflow-nodes (the lanes in the document and the compiler: workflow-engine). _Tránh_: output, handle, Cổng ra, Cổng (alone; that is a gate), exit.
- **Lane** (`lane`) · _Nhánh_ — The steps a step holds for one of its ports, as the canvas and the outline show them: a column under a switch's or an approval's card, a column inside a parallel step's container, a loop's body, or the dashed error lane _Khi lỗi_ in its step's right gutter; an empty lane shows a _Thêm Bước_ slot. Lanes keep document order, which is outline order and keyboard order too (←/→ moves to the sibling lane), and UI text names a lane by its label or its place (_nhánh 2 trên 3_, _Chờ tất cả nhánh xong_). Added 2026-10-24 (V4.24) and flagged for `mattpocock-skills:domain-modeling`. _Owner_: workflow-builder-ui (the lane in the document: workflow-engine). _Tránh_: làn (as the UI term; the docs may say làn, and a worker's lane is always named by its key, as in làn `studio_runs`), swimlane, track, column, path.
- **Edge** (a layout edge, a React Flow `Edge`) · _Đường nối_ — A line the canvas draws between consecutive steps of a sequence and into and out of lanes, at anchor points the layout module computes from the tree; never stored, since the document has no `edges[]` from V4.24 (ADR 0112), and never drawn by a builder. Each edge carries a _Thêm Bước_ slot ("+", named _Thêm Bước sau «tên»_) and a Vietnamese `ariaLabel` or none. Changed 2026-10-24 and flagged for `mattpocock-skills:domain-modeling`. _Owner_: workflow-builder-ui. _Tránh_: link, arrow (except the loop's return arrow _Lặp lại_), transition, connection (that is a provider connection), `edges[]`.
- **Error policy** (`on_error`, with `retry` and `timeout_s`) · _Khi lỗi_ — What a node does once its attempts (`retry`, 1 to 5 with fixed or exponential backoff, and `timeout_s` per attempt, shown as badges such as _Thử lại ×3_ and _Hết giờ 30 s_) are spent: _Dừng lượt chạy_ (`fail`), _Tiếp tục kèm lỗi_ (`continue`, the error written to the node's output), _Đi nhánh lỗi_ (`route`, its error lane _Khi lỗi_, from W1), or _Chờ xử lý_ (`incident`, which parks a `live` run on an incident; see Incident; W1, V4.26). A node's output carries `error` under `continue` and `route` alike (workflow-nodes R1.5), and inside a parallel lane an error lane only merges back, never ends the run (ADR 0112). _Owner_: workflow-nodes. _Tránh_: catch, fallback, try/catch.
- **Loop step** (`logic.loop`, `max_iterations`) · _Bước vòng lặp_ — A node that runs its body, a lane of steps, once and then again while a condition holds (a bounded do-while, drawn as the container _Lặp lại khi … (tối đa N lần)_ with the return arrow _Lặp lại_ on its left), at most the `max_iterations` it must declare, which the plan clamps; at that count the run takes the node's `max_reached` lane _Đã đủ N lần_, and a loop step that declares none fails the run with `error.code` `loop_limit_reached` (V4.P11). Its body may hold waits (a rework loop with an approval), unlike a parallel lane or a map body, and a Loop exit leaves it early. The run's step cap holds across resumes through the node wrapper's counter, since LangGraph's `recursion_limit` restarts on every resume (measured 2026-10-24); the worst-case step count computed at submit only warns. Its effects' idempotency keys carry `iteration_path`. It is the only way a workflow repeats steps, from W1 (V4.26, ADR 0112). Changed 2026-10-24 and flagged for `mattpocock-skills:domain-modeling`. _Owner_: workflow-nodes (the loop's structure in the compiler: workflow-engine). _Tránh_: loop node, while, repeat, cycle, `logic.map` (that runs once per item of a list).
- **Loop exit** (`logic.break`) · _Thoát vòng lặp_ — A node allowed only in a loop step's body and in no parallel lane, even when its loop sits inside that lane, which leaves the loop at once through a conditional edge the compiler adds to the loop's exit; a step after the loop may therefore reference only the body steps that run before every exit (`wf.break_outside_loop` refuses one elsewhere). W1 (V4.26, ADR 0112). Added 2026-10-24 and flagged for `mattpocock-skills:domain-modeling`. _Owner_: workflow-nodes (the exit edge in the compiler: workflow-engine). _Tránh_: break (in UI), Dừng vòng lặp, `flow.stop` (that ends the whole run).
- **Parallel step** (`logic.parallel@1`) · _Bước song song_ — A node whose lanes run at the same time, each compiled as a subgraph with a state schema of its own, so a slow lane never holds the others back, and which continues at its Join point once every lane has finished (join mode all in W1). It is drawn as the container _Song song_, its lanes side by side over the bar _Chờ tất cả nhánh xong_. No lane may hold a wait or a node whose error policy is _Chờ xử lý_ (see Wait; `wf.wait_in_parallel`), nor a Loop exit, even of a loop inside the lane (see Loop exit; `wf.break_outside_loop`), and no step may end the run inside a lane, neither `flow.stop` nor an error lane that ends the run (`wf.stop_in_parallel`), because a run ended inside a lane is reported a success while the join and every later step never run (measured 2026-10-24); two lanes writing one variable are refused at submit (`wf.variable_conflict`). `logic.join` is no node type of its own: it is the end of `logic.parallel`. W1 (V4.26, ADR 0112). Added 2026-10-24, changed 2026-10-25 and flagged for `mattpocock-skills:domain-modeling`. _Owner_: workflow-nodes (the subgraph lanes and the join in the compiler: workflow-engine). _Tránh_: fork, split, fan-out, branch-all, Bước rẽ nhánh (that is `logic.switch`), `logic.map` (that runs once per item of a list).
- **Join point** (the end of `logic.parallel`, one waiting edge) · _Điểm nối_ — Where a parallel step continues once every one of its lanes has finished, drawn as the bar _Chờ tất cả nhánh xong_: the compiler meets the lanes' ends with one waiting edge, which runs the join exactly once per pass of the step, once per iteration inside a loop, where plain edges would run it twice (measured 2026-10-24, ADR 0112). The dot where a switch's or an approval's lanes merge is no Join point, since exactly one of their lanes runs. W1 (V4.26). Added 2026-10-24 and flagged for `mattpocock-skills:domain-modeling`. _Owner_: workflow-engine. _Tránh_: join node, `logic.join` (folded into the parallel step), barrier, merge (in UI), Điểm gộp.
- **Sub-workflow step** (`flow.subworkflow@1`) · _Bước quy trình con_ — A node that runs another workflow as a child run and waits for it on a wait of kind `child_run`, so its type opens a wait: it is refused in a parallel lane (`wf.wait_in_parallel`, like every waiting step) and allowed in a loop's body. The child's version is pinned at submit and never followed (ADR 0109), child runs nest at most three deep (see Chain depth), and cancelling the parent stops every child run within 5 s. It calls only a workflow that declares a `triggers[]` entry of kind `call`, which no other door starts, and submit refuses any other workflow with `wf.subworkflow_not_callable` (Đạt, Q-h6, 2026-10-26). Tier B of W1, which no pilot ticket waits for (V4.33). Added 2026-10-25 and flagged for `mattpocock-skills:domain-modeling`. _Owner_: workflow-nodes (the child run: workflow-engine). _Tránh_: Workflow tool (_Quy trình dùng như công cụ_, a workflow offered to an agent), subflow, call activity, Khung (that is a container).
- **Approval step** (`human.approval`) · _Bước phê duyệt_ — A node that opens an approval and waits, then continues in its outcome lane _Duyệt_ or _Từ chối_. From W1 it may require two or more approvers who must all approve (k = n): still one step with one wait, which takes _Duyệt_ when the last of them approves and _Từ chối_ at the first rejection, closing what the others could still decide, each decision passing the step's guard, so none of them is the run's requester or its run-as principal; k of n with k < n is W3 (V4.26, ADR 0112). It never sits in a parallel lane. In W1 it has no deadline of its own and ends only with the run's deadline; from W2 an expiry takes its _Hết hạn_ lane or, without one, fails the run with `approval_expired`. In a `simulate` run (and a `test` run from W2) it waits for no real decider: the simulated decision comes from the person running it or from a gate case, is audited `studio.step.approval.simulated` and never satisfies an approval of a `live` run (ADR 0111). _Owner_: workflow-nodes. _Tránh_: confirm node, gate (alone).
- **Approval delegation** (`approval delegation`) · _Duyệt thay_ — An approver's hand-over of the approvals assigned to them to another person while they are away, for a set period (from, to) and audited when set, removed and used. The delegate must hold the permission the approval type stamps and `approvals.decide` and must not be the person who started the run; outside those conditions the decision guard still answers 403, as ADR 0104 decision 11 says. From W2 (ADR 0112). _Owner_: workflow-nodes. _Tránh_: ủy quyền (alone), Ủy quyền dùng kết nối (that is a connection delegation), proxy approval.
- **Human task** (`human.task`) · _Việc cần làm_ — A node that assigns a form or job to a person and waits for completion. _Owner_: workflow-nodes. _Tránh_: todo, ticket.
- **Assignee** (`assignee`) · _Người xử lý_ — The person or role a human task is assigned to. _Owner_: workflow-nodes. _Tránh_: owner (for this), handler.
- **Task escalation** (`escalation`) · _Chuyển cấp_ — Reassigning an overdue human task or approval, for example to the assignee's line manager. _Owner_: workflow-nodes. _Tránh_: escalation (alone), privilege escalation.
- **Code node** (`code.python`) · _Bước mã Python_ — A node that runs tenant-written Python in a fresh code sandbox, behind a dedicated permission and plan entitlement. _Owner_: workflow-nodes. _Tránh_: script node, function node.
- **Expression** (`CEL, ExpressionEngine`) · _Biểu thức_ — A CEL expression evaluated server-side, type-checked at submit and cost-bounded. _Owner_: workflow-nodes. _Tránh_: formula (alone), script, code.
- **Text template** (`{{ }}`) · _Chuỗi có biến_ — Text with `{{ }}` CEL placeholders, rendered server-side. _Owner_: workflow-nodes. _Tránh_: template (alone), Jinja, format string.
- **Canvas** (`@xyflow/react`, `apps/web/lib/workflow-layout.ts`) · _Sơ đồ_ — The builder's structured view of a workflow's step tree, drawn top to bottom by `@xyflow/react` (pinned exactly, under the canvas glob only; ADR 0101) with antd drawing every card, and laid out from the tree by a pure layout module, with no layout library and no stored positions: the same document always gives the same coordinates, and inserting a step moves only what follows it in its lane and in the enclosing sequences (a step with lanes may push sibling lanes sideways, never change their vertical or lane order). A builder never positions or wires a step: placing one in a slot (the "+" on an edge, _Thêm Bước_ in an empty lane, or a drag from the palette onto a slot) connects it, nodes are neither draggable nor connectable, and moving a step is a tree operation (V4.24, V4.25). Changed 2026-10-24 and flagged for `mattpocock-skills:domain-modeling`. _Owner_: workflow-builder-ui. _Tránh_: board, diagram editor, flowchart, graph view.
- **Container** (`container`, a layout shape) · _Khung_ — The frame the canvas draws around a step whose lanes sit inside it: a parallel step (_Song song_), a loop step (_Lặp lại khi … (tối đa N lần)_) and, from W2, a map step; a switch and an approval are no containers, their lanes hanging under the step's card. Every container collapses to one card and expands again, a per-viewer state never stored in the document, and deleting one names every step it removes (V4.24). Added 2026-10-24 and flagged for `mattpocock-skills:domain-modeling`. _Owner_: workflow-builder-ui. _Tránh_: khối (as the UI term), block, group, nhóm (that is a team), scope, subflow.
- **Outline view** (`outline view`, an antd Tree) · _Dạng danh sách_ — The workflow's step tree itself as an antd Tree, so it cannot drift from the canvas: the screen-reader path, and the primary editor below `md` and at 320 px. Every Tree node carries `aria-level`, `aria-posinset` and `aria-setsize`, which antd 6.6.5's Tree does not emit on its own, under a test that goes red without them (V4.24). _Owner_: workflow-builder-ui. _Tránh_: tree view.
- **Variable picker** (`variable picker`) · _Chọn biến_ — The UI that inserts a typed path to an earlier node's output into an expression. _Owner_: workflow-builder-ui. _Tránh_: field picker.
- **Run request** (`RunRequestService`) · _Yêu cầu chạy_ — The one admission path that turns a trigger, chat or API call into a queued run. _Owner_: workflow-engine. _Tránh_: enqueue, start call.
- **Wait** (`platform.run_waits`) · _Điểm chờ_ — A parked run waiting for a time, an event, a human decision or, from W1, a person's resolution of an incident (kind `incident`, see Incident) or of an effect whose outcome is unknown (kind `outcome_review`, see Outcome review, V4.30), and in tier B for a child run (kind `child_run`, see Sub-workflow step, V4.33). No wait sits in a parallel lane or a map body; a loop's body may hold one (ADR 0112). Changed 2026-10-25 and flagged for `mattpocock-skills:domain-modeling`. _Owner_: workflow-engine. _Tránh_: sleep, pause (alone).
- **Incident** (`platform.run_waits` kind `incident`, `on_error: incident`) · _Sự cố_ — The wait a `live` run parks on when a node whose error policy is _Chờ xử lý_ has spent its attempts: the run stays `waiting`, bounded by its deadline (`deadline_at`, V4.14), until a person resolves it through the decision path with _Chạy tiếp từ Bước lỗi_, which re-queues the run, or _Kết thúc_, which ends it `failed`. In any other mode nobody is assigned to resolve one, so that node fails the run as under _Dừng lượt chạy_ (ADR 0112 decision 5). A `failed` run stays terminal for every lane that acts on finished runs (checkpoints, the budget lease), so nothing reopens one. A call whose outcome is unknown never becomes an incident: it parks on an outcome review first, and only its _Bỏ_ fails the node into its error policy (see Outcome review, V4.30). Modelled on Camunda's incidents. W1 (V4.26, ADR 0112, ADR 0113). Added 2026-10-24, changed 2026-10-25 and flagged for `mattpocock-skills:domain-modeling`. _Owner_: workflow-engine (the run-history screen: telemetry-and-audit). _Tránh_: Sự cố dữ liệu cá nhân (that is a personal-data incident), Đối soát tác động không rõ kết quả (that is an outcome review), error (alone), failed run, ticket, alert.
- **Resume from failed step** (resolving an `incident` wait) · _Chạy tiếp từ Bước lỗi_ — The resolution of an incident that re-queues its run and runs the failed node again in the same run: the same run id keeps the node's idempotency key, so an effect the platform recorded as done is not repeated, and the run keeps its pinned binding set while each call takes its connection's latest unrevoked secret version (V4.35, ADR 0113), so it recovers a transient fault or one a secret rotation fixed, never a wrong binding value, which the screen says; when the failed attempt's outcome is unknown, the screen shows _kết quả lần trước không rõ_ before the person chooses. It has a permission of its own and cross-tenant negative tests like every run route. W1 (V4.26, ADR 0112, ADR 0113). Added 2026-10-24, changed 2026-10-25 and flagged for `mattpocock-skills:domain-modeling`. _Owner_: workflow-engine (the screen: telemetry-and-audit). _Tránh_: retry (that is a node's attempts), Thử lại, re-run, Replay (_Chạy lại_, a new run), Simulated replay (_Chạy lại mô phỏng_, a new `simulate` run), Fork and _Chạy tách từ bước_ (dropped, see Simulated replay), reopen.
- **Outcome review** (`platform.run_waits` kind `outcome_review`, `runs.reconcile`) · _Đối soát tác động không rõ kết quả_ — The wait a run parks on when an outbound call ended `outcome_unknown`, a call that may have reached the provider (a timeout or a reset after sending, an ambiguous 5xx, an SMTP `DATA` sent with no 250 reply), and its Action's `on_unknown` is `review`, as every Action's is in W1 (see Delivery guarantee). The run stays `waiting`, with no new run status, and its run history shows the step _Không rõ nhà cung cấp đã nhận hay chưa_ with `reason_code` `outcome_unknown`. A holder of `runs.reconcile`, which workflow-engine registers and only the tenant owner holds by default (H-8), chooses _Đã nhận_ (the call is recorded `succeeded` with evidence), _Chưa nhận, gửi lại cùng khóa_ or _Bỏ_ (the node fails and follows its error policy), and for a `critical` effect that person is not the run's requester (D11); the decision resolves the wait and queues the run in one transaction, as an approval's does (ADR 0113). An unknown outcome never enters an error lane nor becomes `continue`'s error. At the run's deadline an unresolved one ends the run like any overdue run: its tool execution keeps `outcome_unknown`, the ending lane audits `run.effect_unresolved`, and the run history shows the step _Không rõ nhà cung cấp đã nhận hay chưa, chưa ai xác nhận_ for good (H-6); when a kill or a freeze cancels such a run, the same audit names the person who set it, with the lane in `details.lane` (H-22). W1 settles nothing automatically: the `studio_reconcile` lane, which calls an Action's lookup, comes in W2 with the first connector that declares one (H-5). No outcome review opens inside a parallel lane, where no wait sits (ADR 0112): submit refuses an effect step whose Action's `on_unknown` is `review` inside a lane (Đạt, Q-h5, 2026-10-26). W1 (V4.30). Added 2026-10-25 and flagged for `mattpocock-skills:domain-modeling`. _Owner_: workflow-engine (the run-history screen: telemetry-and-audit). _Tránh_: Sự cố (that is an incident), retry, manual retry, reconciliation (alone), `outcome_unknown` (that is the call's result, not the wait).
- **Blocked run** (`status='blocked'`) · _Bị chặn khi tiếp tục_ — A run that could not resume because a check refused it (such as a frozen version). _Owner_: workflow-engine. _Tránh_: stuck run, failed run.
- **Dead-lettered run** (`status='dead_lettered'`) · _Hỏng sau nhiều lần thử_ — A run that exhausted its retries and needs a person: `studio_run_reaper` re-queues a run whose claim expired and dead-letters it after five attempts (ADR 0113). A node's own spent attempts are its error policy's to answer, under _Chờ xử lý_ with an incident (see Error policy, Incident). _Owner_: workflow-engine. _Tránh_: dead run, poison run, Sự cố (that is a run waiting on one node's spent attempts).
- **Replay** (`replay`) · _Chạy lại_ — Starting a new run with the same input on a chosen version. _Owner_: workflow-engine. _Tránh_: re-run, retry, Chạy tiếp từ Bước lỗi (that continues the same run).
- **Simulated replay** (a new run, mode `simulate`) · _Chạy lại mô phỏng_ — Starting a new `simulate` run of the same pinned version with the same input as a past run, from W2. It replaces Fork (_Chạy tách từ bước_, starting a new run from a past run's checkpoint at a chosen step), dropped because re-running a run from a step it had passed would send that step's later effects again under the new run's keys (V4.36, H-13); continuing a run in place is _Chạy tiếp từ Bước lỗi_ (V4.26). Added 2026-10-25 and flagged for `mattpocock-skills:domain-modeling`. _Owner_: workflow-engine. _Tránh_: Fork, Chạy tách từ bước, fork from step, branch, clone run, Chạy tiếp từ Bước lỗi (that continues the same run).
- **Chain depth** (`chain_depth`) · _Độ sâu chuỗi_ — How many runs deep a run sits in a chain of runs starting runs, with two caps that stop loops: 3 for child runs (sub-workflows, workflow tools, linked agents, agent nodes) and 8 for a chain of record events. _Owner_: workflow-engine. _Tránh_: recursion level, nesting.
- **Trigger** (`triggers[]`) · _Trình kích hoạt_ — What starts a workflow: a record event, a schedule, a Data Hub endpoint or a manual start. _Owner_: workflow-triggers. _Tránh_: event (alone), hook, starter.
- **Trigger delivery** (`studio.trigger_deliveries`) · _Lần kích hoạt_ — One firing of one trigger for one version, deduplicated, with its outcome. _Owner_: workflow-triggers. _Tránh_: delivery (alone), invocation.
- **Data Hub endpoint** (`kind='endpoint'`) · _Nguồn dữ liệu_ — A tenant inbound URL that accepts signed events into a workflow (ADR 0008, Standard Webhooks). It is a kind without a quality gate, like an object or a tool: a version goes from Nháp through Chờ duyệt to Đang dùng once a second person approves it; submitting one takes `studio.datahub.manage`, and who may approve it is answered by `studio.publish.approve`, as for every kind but an MCP tool (ADR 0120). _Owner_: workflow-triggers. _Tránh_: webhook (alone), API endpoint, Data Hub (alone).
- **Inbound event** (`studio.inbound_events`) · _Sự kiện nhận_ — One accepted request to a Data Hub endpoint or provider-app webhook, stored before processing. _Owner_: workflow-triggers. _Tránh_: webhook payload, message.
- **Quarantine** (`status='quarantined'`) · _Cách ly_ — Keeping an inbound event that failed its schema check, stored with a readable error and never processed; from W2 the integrator can replay it (_phát lại_) from the event history, which is not a run's Replay. Only a schema failure is quarantined: an event with a bad signature is refused (401) and never stored. _Owner_: workflow-triggers. _Tránh_: reject queue, spam, quarantine (for a signature failure).
- **Schedule** (`schedule binding`) · _Lịch chạy_ — A trigger firing at times given by a schedule binding, in the tenant's time zone. _Owner_: workflow-triggers. _Tránh_: cron (in UI), timer.
- **Internal form** (`trigger.manual form`) · _Biểu mẫu nội bộ_ — A trigger built on `trigger.manual`: an antd form generated from a workflow's `input_schema`, open to members holding `studio.workflows.run`, beside a "Chạy quy trình" button on a record page (W2). _Owner_: workflow-triggers (the record-page button: custom-objects). _Tránh_: web form (that feeds a Data Hub endpoint), public form, survey.

## Tools, skills and MCP

- **Tool** (`Tool (tenant data)`) · _Công cụ_ — A tenant-defined, versioned tool of one kind compiled to a tool spec; executed only through the ToolExecutor. _Owner_: tools-and-skills. _Tránh_: function, action (alone), plugin.
- **Tool kind** (`kind: http, openapi, mcp, workflow, object_crud, code, builtin`) · _Loại công cụ_ — The kind that selects the generic factory building a tool spec and its policy floor. _Owner_: tools-and-skills. _Tránh_: tool type.
- **Tool contract** (`contract`) · _Hợp đồng công cụ_ — A tool version's input and output schemas, side-effect level and idempotency. _Owner_: tools-and-skills. _Tránh_: signature, interface.
- **Tool policy** (`policy`) · _Chính sách công cụ_ — The tenant's approval and limit settings on a tool, which may tighten, never loosen, the kind floor. _Owner_: tools-and-skills. _Tránh_: rule, permission (for this).
- **Tool implementation** (`implementation`) · _Cách thực thi_ — What a tool version calls: an HTTP request, an MCP tool, a workflow, a record operation or code. _Owner_: tools-and-skills. _Tránh_: binding (that is a declared need), target.
- **Kind floor** (`KIND_FLOORS`) · _Mức sàn theo loại_ — The minimum policy a tool kind always carries, which tenants cannot loosen. _Owner_: tools-and-skills. _Tránh_: default policy, baseline.
- **Output trust** (`output_trust`) · _Độ tin cậy kết quả_ — Whether a tool's output is trusted or untrusted content; untrusted output taints the run. _Owner_: tools-and-skills. _Tránh_: safety level.
- **Run taint** (`tainted_at`) · _Đã tiếp xúc nội dung ngoài_ — The mark on a run that has read untrusted content, after which high-impact tools need approval. _Owner_: tools-and-skills. _Tránh_: context taint, Ngữ cảnh đã nhiễm.
- **Lethal trifecta** (`trifecta`) · _Bộ ba nguy hiểm_ — Private data access, untrusted content and an outbound channel on one path of an agent or workflow version; a version where such a path reaches the effect with no person before it is refused at submit (422). _Owner_: compliance-pdpl. _Tránh_: Bộ ba rủi ro, Rule of Two (that is a pack design choice).
- **Toolkit** (`namespace`) · _Nhóm công cụ_ — A namespace grouping a provider's tools in the UI. _Owner_: tools-and-skills. _Tránh_: toolset (that is versioned), category.
- **Model-facing view** (`model-view`) · _Bản mô hình nhìn thấy_ — The name, description and schema of a tool as sent to the model, narrowed by field security. _Owner_: tools-and-skills. _Tránh_: prompt schema.
- **Object tool** (`object_crud`) · _Công cụ dữ liệu đối tượng_ — A tool kind that reads or writes records of one object under the run's access. _Owner_: tools-and-skills. _Tránh_: CRUD tool, DB tool.
- **Workflow tool** (`kind='workflow'`) · _Quy trình dùng như công cụ_ — A published workflow offered to an agent as a tool. _Owner_: tools-and-skills. _Tránh_: sub-workflow (for this).
- **Platform tool** (`builtin`) · _Công cụ nền tảng_ — A tool shipped by the platform and offered to tenants read-only. _Owner_: tools-and-skills. _Tránh_: system tool, native tool.
- **Skill** (`SKILL.md`) · _Kỹ năng_ — An Agent Skills package of instructions (text only in v1) served read-only per tenant. _Owner_: tools-and-skills. _Tránh_: prompt snippet, plugin, capability.
- **Pinned skill** (`pinned_skills`) · _Kỹ năng ghim_ — A skill always loaded into an agent's context rather than disclosed on demand. _Owner_: tools-and-skills. _Tránh_: default skill.
- **MCP server registration** (`McpServer`) · _Máy chủ MCP_ — A tenant's registration of an MCP server from the platform catalogue (any URL only from v1.5), with auth, allowed tools and pinned definitions. _Owner_: mcp-client. _Tránh_: MCP connection, plugin.
- **MCP gateway** (`mcp-gateway`) · _Cổng MCP_ — The isolated service holding no secrets at rest through which every MCP call leaves. _Owner_: mcp-client. _Tránh_: MCP proxy, gateway (alone).
- **Pinned tool definition** (`pinned_definition_sha256`) · _Định nghĩa đã ghim_ — The SHA-256 of an MCP tool's definition at admin approval; a change is drift needing re-approval. _Owner_: mcp-client. _Tránh_: definition hash (that is the version hash).
- **Tool approval** (`studio.mcp.tool_approval`) · _Duyệt công cụ_ — The second-person approval of an MCP tool definition, which moves its tool version from Chờ duyệt to Đang dùng before agents may call it; a suspended tool is held by a kill switch, not by a status. _Owner_: mcp-client. _Tránh_: tool review, whitelisting.
- **Server hints** (`annotations`) · _Gợi ý của máy chủ_ — An MCP server's self-declared tool annotations, informational only and never trusted for policy. _Owner_: mcp-client. _Tránh_: tool flags.
- **Elicitation** (`mcp.elicitation`) · _Yêu cầu bổ sung thông tin_ — An MCP server's request for user input, routed to the human-task inbox (v1.5). _Owner_: mcp-client. _Tránh_: prompt (alone), question.

## Connections and egress

- **Connection** (`platform.connections`) · _Kết nối_ — An authorized link from one workspace (or one user) to one account at an external provider, holding its credentials in the vault. _Owner_: connections-and-vault. _Tránh_: credential, integration, account, tài khoản liên kết.
- **Connector** (`connector_key`) · _Trình kết nối_ — A provider capability described by a versioned manifest: auth scheme, actions, triggers, hosts, data location. _Owner_: connections-and-vault. _Tránh_: plugin, app, integration.
- **Connector manifest** (`dw.connector/v1`) · _Bản mô tả trình kết nối_ — The versioned YAML artifact defining a connector; the platform layer ships in `configs/connectors/`. _Owner_: connections-and-vault. _Tránh_: spec (alone), definition.
- **Action** (`<connector>.<action>@<semver>`) · _Hành động_ — One provider operation compiled to a tool spec, carrying a side-effect level, an effect class and, when its side-effect level is not `none`, a `delivery` block that fixes its delivery guarantee (see Delivery guarantee, V4.30). Changed 2026-10-25 and flagged for `mattpocock-skills:domain-modeling`. _Owner_: connections-and-vault. _Tránh_: endpoint, API call.
- **Effect class** (`effect_class`) · _(internal — not shown in the UI)_ — The class of effect a connector manifest declares for each action, required on every one; `message` marks an action that sends a message to a person, so the ToolExecutor runs the send floor before it (ADR 0116, ADR 0124). It is not the side-effect level, which says what a call changes and drives approval and run-mode interception. _Owner_: connections-and-vault. _Tránh_: message flag, side-effect level (for this).
- **Delivery guarantee** (`delivery` in `dw.connector/v1`: `idempotency`, `reconciliation`, `on_unknown`) · _Mức bảo đảm giao nhận_ — What a run promises about one effect when a worker stops or a call goes unanswered, named by level, never by a code, so that no level reads as a `G<n>` line or a spike threshold: _nội bộ_ (a run's state change, a wait, a decision, an outbox event or a record write, `update` and `assign` included: exactly once, since a node's key is stored in the write's transaction and its stored result replayed before its inputs are computed again); _có khóa hay tra cứu_ (the provider applies the effect exactly once within its `retention_hours`, every attempt sending the same key, a hash of the node's `wf:` key, when the Action declares an `idempotency.mode` of `provider_key` or `natural_key` or a `reconciliation.mode` of `lookup`); _không khóa_ (an Action with neither, such as `smtp@1.0.0`: sent at most once automatically, an unknown outcome parking the run on an outcome review for a person to decide, and a `Message-ID` derived from the key only a hint to the receiving mailbox); and _đồng thời_ (two calls with one key never both send while the first holds its claim). It holds across crashes because the executor commits a `dispatched` row in `platform.tool_executions` before the call leaves, so a takeover of an expired claim reruns the call only where no `dispatched` row exists and otherwise follows `on_unknown`. Every Action whose side-effect level is not `none` declares the block; `on_unknown` is `review` (the value when absent), `resend_same_key` or `lookup_then_resend`, and no value treats an unknown outcome as a failure. W1 has the internal, unkeyed and concurrent levels only: its loader refuses any `idempotency.mode` or `reconciliation.mode` but `none` and any `on_unknown` but `review` until the first connector that declares a key or a lookup arrives in W2, with `resend_same_key`, `lookup_then_resend` and the `studio_reconcile` lane (V4.30, H-5). Added 2026-10-25 and flagged for `mattpocock-skills:domain-modeling`. _Owner_: connections-and-vault (the dispatch log and the takeover: runtime-hardening; the node wrapper and the outcome review: workflow-engine). _Tránh_: exactly once (alone), at least once, effect risk, `effect_risk`, G1 to G4 (the review answer's codes).
- **Provider app** (`provider_app_key`) · _Ứng dụng nhà cung cấp_ — The OAuth client and webhook subscription registered at a provider: in v1 the platform's own Meta and Zalo apps, while for Google the tenant uses its own internal client and the platform holds none (DO-20). _Owner_: connections-and-vault. _Tránh_: app (alone), BYO app.
- **OAuth flow** (`platform.oauth_flows`) · _Phiên cấp quyền_ — One authorization-code attempt with hashed state, PKCE verifier, expiry and initiator. _Owner_: connections-and-vault. _Tránh_: session (alone).
- **Connection status** (`pending, active, needs_reauth, disabled, revoked`) · _Đang chờ / Hoạt động / Cần kết nối lại / Đã tắt / Đã thu hồi_ — The stored state of a connection; expiring is derived, never stored. _Owner_: connections-and-vault. _Tránh_: error (as a status), expired.
- **Share scope** (`share_scope: owner, workspace, workspace_subtree`) · _Phạm vi dùng chung_ — Where in the workspace tree a connection may be used. _Owner_: connections-and-vault. _Tránh_: visibility, access depth (that is for records).
- **Delegation** (`platform.connection_delegations`) · _Ủy quyền dùng kết nối_ — An owner's expiring, revocable permission for one workflow or agent to use their personal connection in background runs. _Owner_: connections-and-vault. _Tránh_: Ủy quyền (alone), impersonation, Duyệt thay (that is an approval delegation).
- **Vault** (`SecretStore, platform.secret_envelopes`) · _Kho bí mật_ — The store of tenant secrets as AES-256-GCM envelopes, readable only through the credential broker. _Owner_: connections-and-vault. _Tránh_: secret manager, Vault (product name).
- **Secret** (`Secret`) · _Thông tin bí mật_ — A value that grants access or signs messages; write-only in the API and shown as configured. A connection's secret has versions and no run pins one: each call uses the connection's latest unrevoked version and records the one it used (`credential_version` on `platform.tool_executions`; V4.35, H-10). A rotation adds a version, and `credential_purge` deletes the replaced one 24 hours later; revoking a version for compromise deletes it at once and stops every unfinished run of the connection at its next outbound call with `credential_revoked`, even when a newer version exists. A Data Hub endpoint's signing secret keeps V4.20's 24-hour overlap. Changed 2026-10-25 and flagged for `mattpocock-skills:domain-modeling`. _Owner_: connections-and-vault. _Tránh_: password, key (alone).
- **Tenant key** (`KEK, tenant-<uuid> in Transit`) · _Khóa của công ty_ — The per-tenant OpenBao Transit key that wraps data keys and is destroyed at offboarding. _Owner_: connections-and-vault. _Tránh_: master key.
- **Crypto-shredding** (`destroy tenant key`) · _Xóa bằng hủy khóa_ — Destroying a tenant key so that tenant's ciphertext becomes unreadable everywhere, backups included, once the oldest key-store backup still holding the key has expired; key-store backups are therefore kept no longer than data backups. _Owner_: connections-and-vault. _Tránh_: xóa bí mật, Hủy khóa (alone).
- **Egress client** (`EgressClient, EgressPort`) · _(internal — not shown in the UI)_ — The only HTTP client for tenant-directed traffic: address policy, IP pinning, allowlists, limits, breakers, spans. _Owner_: connections-and-vault. _Tránh_: HTTP client, proxy.
- **Egress proxy** (`smokescreen`) · _(internal — not shown in the UI)_ — The network-level second layer on its own network refusing non-global addresses and hosts outside its role. _Owner_: connections-and-vault. _Tránh_: firewall, proxy (alone).
- **Allowed host** (`platform.egress_allowed_hosts`) · _Máy chủ được phép_ — A host a tenant admin allows for tenant-defined destinations (HTTP tools, MCP, webhooks, BYOM endpoints). _Owner_: connections-and-vault. _Tránh_: whitelist.
- **Outbound webhook endpoint** (`platform.webhook_endpoints`) · _Webhook gửi đi_ — A tenant-registered HTTPS URL subscribed to event types and signed per Standard Webhooks; it carries thin events unless personal data is enabled for that endpoint, with audit, hidden values and the residency route applied. _Owner_: connections-and-vault. _Tránh_: callback, hook.
- **Webhook delivery** (`platform.webhook_deliveries`) · _Lượt gửi webhook_ — One send of one event to one outbound endpoint with its attempts and outcome. _Owner_: connections-and-vault. _Tránh_: delivery (alone), message.
- **Signature verifier** (`SignatureVerifier`) · _Bộ kiểm chữ ký_ — A pure function checking an inbound request's signature by scheme, shared with workflow triggers. _Owner_: connections-and-vault. _Tránh_: auth (alone).

## Lifecycle and evaluation

- **Quality gate** (`gate`) · _Cổng chất lượng_ — The submit-time check an agent or workflow candidate must pass before its approval: the platform security suite at 100% from W1; for a workflow, also from W1, its deterministic scenario cases (an input, simulated decisions, mock outputs pinned per step, an expected outcome and CEL assertions, graded without a model; V4.37), a failing case failing the gate run; and the tenant's evaluation set at the gate policy's pass rate from W2. A kind without a gate (an object, a tool, a Data Hub endpoint) goes straight to approval. Changed 2026-10-25 and flagged for `mattpocock-skills:domain-modeling`. _Owner_: agent-lifecycle. _Tránh_: test step, CI, W1 exit gate (that is a planning gate).
- **W1 exit gate** (`E1`, `E2`, `E3`) · _(internal — a planning term, not shown in the UI)_ — One of three gates the W1 work passes before what each guards: E1 before the first effect, in every profile; E2 before the first deployed environment, the uat of exit W1b; E3 before real personal data, being the shadow-run conditions of YC99. W1 has two exits: W1a, the pilot on profile `test` with synthetic leads (`marketing-pilot/05`), behind E1, and W1b, uat with OpenBao and the read-only Marketing assistant (`marketing-pilot/06`), behind E2 as well (V4.31). The area file defines the labels, and what each gate holds is listed once, in Phương án A of the area's `requirements.md`. They are named E rather than the review answer's G-a to G-c so that none reads as a `G<n>` line. Added 2026-10-25 and flagged for `mattpocock-skills:domain-modeling`. _Owner_: the studio area file (`.claude/plans/studio.md`; each gate's contents: `requirements.md`). _Tránh_: G-a, G-b, G-c, Quality gate (_Cổng chất lượng_, an artifact's submit-time check), Port (_Nhánh ra_), P0 gate.
- **Gate run** (`gate_run`) · _Lượt kiểm định_ — One execution of the quality gate on one candidate's definition hash, a `studio.gate_runs` row of purpose `gate`, whose trials are `simulate` runs reading under a provisional boundary derived from the sealed candidate (see Boundary); a row of purpose `dry` is a dry evaluation. _Owner_: agent-lifecycle. _Tránh_: test run (that is run mode test).
- **Dry evaluation** (`purpose dry`) · _Kiểm định thử_ — An evaluation of an agent's or a workflow's draft that cannot lead to publish: a holder of `studio.evals.run` asks for it through agent-lifecycle's `POST .../versions/{version_id}/dry-evaluations`, and it is recorded as a `studio.gate_runs` row of purpose `dry`. Its trials are `simulate` runs, the only mode that may run a draft (see Draft): each pins the draft's provisional hash in its run manifest and runs the graph compiled under that hash, cached under a key that includes it (ADR 0112 for a workflow, ADR 0117 for an agent), since a draft keeps its id while it is edited. _Owner_: agent-lifecycle. _Tránh_: preview test.
- **Gate policy** (`GatePolicy`) · _Chính sách cổng_ — The numbers a gate applies: minimum cases, pass rate, k, cost ceilings. _Owner_: agent-lifecycle. _Tránh_: threshold config.
- **Evaluation set** (`eval_dataset (purpose gate)`) · _Bộ đề kiểm định_ — A tenant's versioned set of cases a version must pass at submit. _Owner_: agent-lifecycle. _Tránh_: test set, golden set, Question bank.
- **Question bank** (`purpose question_bank`) · _Ngân hàng câu hỏi_ — A tenant's pool of sample questions used to draft cases and for online evaluation. _Owner_: agent-lifecycle. _Tránh_: evaluation set.
- **Case** (`eval_case`) · _Câu kiểm định_ — One input with expectations in an evaluation set. _Owner_: agent-lifecycle. _Tránh_: test case (in UI), sample.
- **pass^k** (`pass_k`) · _Đạt cả k lần_ — A case passes only if all k trials pass. _Owner_: agent-lifecycle. _Tránh_: pass@k.
- **Platform security suite** (`studio_security_suite`) · _Bộ kiểm định an ninh nền tảng_ — The platform-owned prompt-injection, cross-tenant and missing-evidence cases every version must pass at 100%. _Owner_: agent-lifecycle. _Tránh_: red-team set.
- **Grader** (`grader_key`) · _Bộ chấm_ — A deterministic or judge-based function scoring an observation against a case. _Owner_: agent-lifecycle. _Tránh_: scorer, evaluator.
- **Judge rubric** (`judge_rubric_version`) · _Tiêu chí chấm_ — The versioned rubric a model judge applies. _Owner_: agent-lifecycle. _Tránh_: prompt (alone).
- **Online evaluation** (`online evaluation`) · _Kiểm định trực tuyến_ — Sampled grading of live runs, off by default and opt-in per version. _Owner_: agent-lifecycle. _Tránh_: monitoring (alone).
- **Capability analysis** (`studio.version_assessments`) · _Phân tích năng lực_ — The analysis, run when an agent or workflow version is submitted, of what it can reach (data, untrusted input, outbound channels): it refuses a lethal trifecta path at submit with 422, suggests an AI risk tier and stores its result, which the gate lane only reads as a backstop. _Owner_: compliance-pdpl. _Tránh_: capability_report, CapabilityReport, risk scan.
- **AI risk tier** (`ai_risk_tier`) · _Mức rủi ro AI_ — The AI Law 134/2025/QH15 risk class recorded on a version; a tenant may not declare below the suggestion in v1. _Owner_: compliance-pdpl. _Tránh_: risk level (alone).
- **Control switch** (`control_switch`) · _Công tắc điều khiển_ — A freeze or kill switch checked at ingress, admission, every model call and every tool call. _Owner_: agent-lifecycle. _Tránh_: feature flag, toggle.
- **Freeze** (`freeze`) · _Đóng băng_ — A system- or tenant-wide stop of new effects; running runs cancel at the next check, a call already in flight being recorded by its egress client's typed result (V4.30, ADR 0120), a new interactive run (chat, manual, test, replay, evaluation) is refused at once with `tenant_frozen`, and a queued run a trigger started (Data Hub endpoint, provider app, schedule, record event) is held like a held inbound event and runs in order once the freeze lifts. A freeze has no time limit, so the retention lane skips the inbound events it holds and counts them; lifting a tenant freeze takes a second person (`studio.lifecycle.unfreeze`, ADR 0120). Changed 2026-10-25 and flagged for `mattpocock-skills:domain-modeling`. _Owner_: agent-lifecycle. _Tránh_: pause (alone), maintenance mode.
- **Kill** (`kill`) · _Tắt khẩn cấp_ — An immediate stop of one agent, workflow or tool, set by one person with an audit event; lifting it needs a second person. _Owner_: agent-lifecycle. _Tránh_: disable, delete.
- **Revive** (`studio.lifecycle.revive`) · _Bật lại_ — Lifting a kill through a strict second-person approval. _Owner_: agent-lifecycle. _Tránh_: restore, Khôi phục (that is undelete).
- **Run halted** (`RunHaltedError`) · _Lượt chạy bị dừng_ — A run stopped at a check by freeze, kill or a hard limit. _Owner_: agent-lifecycle. _Tránh_: crashed, aborted (in UI).

- **Usage dashboard** (`usage dashboard`) · _Mức độ sử dụng_ — The tenant page showing usage and cost against limits, read from the usage ledger. _Owner_: usage-and-limits. _Tránh_: billing page, analytics.

## Marketing pilot (first domain pack)

- **Pilot** (`marketing pilot`) · _Thí điểm_ — The first vertical slice: Marketing lead intake to an approved Zalo or email message (D4). _Owner_: marketing-pilot. _Tránh_: POC, trial.
- **Synthetic data** (`synthetic data`) · _Dữ liệu giả lập_ — Generated data containing no real person, used until a data-processing agreement is signed. _Owner_: marketing-pilot. _Tránh_: test data, fake data.
- **Shadow run** (`shadow run`) · _Chạy bóng_ — Running the pilot beside the customer's current process without sending anything. _Owner_: marketing-pilot. _Tránh_: parallel run, dry run.
- **Lead** (`lead (object mkt_lead)`) · _Lead_ — A prospective customer record created from a form, Facebook or Zalo. _Owner_: marketing-pilot. _Tránh_: contact, prospect, khách hàng tiềm năng (in UI).
- **Lead source** (`lead_source`) · _Nguồn lead_ — Where a lead came from (form, Meta Lead Ads, Zalo OA). _Owner_: marketing-pilot. _Tránh_: channel (alone), origin.
- **Campaign** (`campaign`) · _Chiến dịch_ — A marketing campaign leads are attributed to. _Owner_: marketing-pilot. _Tránh_: program.
- **Touchpoint** (`platform.marketing_send_log row` at W1, object `mkt_touchpoint` from W2) · _Tương tác_ — One recorded interaction with a lead, in two waves: at W1 each message dispatched or blocked is one row of the send log (`outcome` `dispatching` or `blocked`, the delivery result being in `platform.tool_executions`) that compliance-pdpl owns, and the pilot's W1 criteria check that log; from W2 the object `mkt_touchpoint`, with a lookup to its lead, arrives with lookup fields. _Owner_: marketing-pilot (the term and the W2 object; the W1 send log is compliance-pdpl's). _Tránh_: activity, event (alone).
- **Lead score and tier** (`lead_score, lead_tier: hot, warm, cold, unknown`) · _Điểm lead, Hạng lead: Nóng / Ấm / Lạnh / Chưa rõ_ — The AI-produced score and its tier, validated into a schema with evidence. _Owner_: marketing-pilot. _Tránh_: rating, grade.
- **Assignment rule** (`assignment_rule`) · _Quy tắc phân bổ_ — The rule choosing which sales rep or team a qualified lead is assigned to. _Owner_: marketing-pilot. _Tránh_: routing rule, round robin (alone).
- **Dedupe** (`dedupe`) · _Kiểm trùng_ — Detecting an existing lead by normalised phone or email before creating a new one. _Owner_: marketing-pilot. _Tránh_: merge (alone), dedup.
- **Outcome event** (`outcome`, a workflow's `outcomes[]`) · _Kết quả_ — A business result attributed to a run (qualified, contacted, won) used for cost per outcome. A workflow declares its outcomes, each a name with a CEL condition, and when a run ends workflow-engine writes the names whose condition holds on the final state into the run's `outcomes` (H-14), which a scenario case's expected outcome is compared with (V4.37); filtering runs by outcome is W2. Changed 2026-10-25 and flagged for `mattpocock-skills:domain-modeling`. _Owner_: marketing-pilot (the run's recorded outcomes: workflow-engine). _Tránh_: conversion, KPI.
